The Aether Engine

A monolithic floating computational citadel projected mathematically in true 3D axonometry. Featuring multi-tier stalactite keels, fortified bastions, quantum foundries, orbital halos, and the focal Aether Core.

Mathematical Purity

True Isometric Projection

Every element is governed by iso(x,y,z) = [x − y, (x + y)/2 − z]. Zero perspective warping. Parallel lines remain strictly parallel across all axes.

Visual Depth System

Dual-Tone Shading & Stroke

Faces use opaque base fills under tone overlays with distinct inner edge (0.8px) and outer silhouette (1.2px) strokes to generate authentic structural volume.

Editorial Restraint

Single Accent Focal Hierarchy

The warm tangerine accent (#f08a59) is reserved exclusively for the central Aether Core and its orbital halos, maintaining strict typographic discipline.
